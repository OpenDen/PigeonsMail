Privacy Policy for PigeonMail

Last updated: November 10, 2026

PigeonMail is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our application.

Information We Collect

PigeonMail is a desktop email client. It does not operate a server that receives your email. The information the application handles is:

  • Account information: the email address of each account you add, and the mail server settings for that account.
  • Mailbox data: messages, folders, attachments, and related metadata that the application reads from your mail provider (Gmail or Microsoft Outlook) so you can view and search them.
  • Outgoing mail: messages you compose and send through your mail provider's SMTP server.
  • Authorization data: OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by Google or Microsoft when you sign in.
  • Local settings: preferences, mail rules, and similar options you configure in the application.

Google User Data

When you sign in with a Google Account, PigeonMail requests the https://mail.google.com/ scope. This scope provides the IMAP and SMTP access needed to read, search, organize, and send your Gmail messages. PigeonMail uses this data only to provide the email features you use in the application: displaying your mail, searching it, marking messages read, starring, archiving, or deleting them, composing and sending messages, and applying your own mail rules.

PigeonMail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. PigeonMail does not use Google user data for advertising, does not sell it, and does not allow humans to read it, except where necessary for security purposes, to comply with applicable law, or with your explicit consent.

How Information Is Used

Information is used only to operate the features described above. Your mail is not used to build profiles, for targeted advertising, or for any purpose unrelated to managing your email.

Information Sharing

PigeonMail does not sell, rent, or share your personal information or email content with third parties. Data is transmitted only to the services needed to perform actions you request:

  • Google and Microsoft: to sign you in, refresh your access, read your mailbox over IMAP, and send mail over SMTP.
  • Remote image hosts: if you choose to load images in an email, the image is requested directly from the sender's server. That server can see your IP address, as with any web request. PigeonMail blocks requests to local and private network addresses.

Local Storage and Encryption

All processing happens on your device. Your mail is cached in a local database that is encrypted with SQLCipher. The database encryption key, and the OAuth tokens for each account, are stored in your operating system's secure keyring (such as Windows Credential Manager). They are not written to plaintext files.

Data Security

PigeonMail protects your information in the following ways:

  • Mail is stored in an encrypted local database, and the encryption key is kept in your operating system's keyring.
  • Sign-in uses OAuth 2.0 with PKCE through your browser. PigeonMail never sees or stores your Google or Microsoft password.
  • OAuth tokens are used only to communicate directly with Google or Microsoft mail servers.
  • Connections to mail servers use encrypted transport.
  • PigeonMail does not write passwords, tokens, encryption keys, or message contents to log files.

Data Retention and Deletion

Mail data and tokens remain on your device until you remove them. You can:

  • Remove an account in PigeonMail to delete its stored credentials and local mail data.
  • Revoke PigeonMail's access to your Google Account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. You can also revoke Microsoft access from your Microsoft account security settings.
  • Uninstall PigeonMail and delete its local data. Because PigeonMail keeps no copy of your data on a server, nothing further needs to be removed remotely.

Children's Privacy

PigeonMail is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.
