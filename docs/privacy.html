Privacy Policy for PigeonMail

Last updated: November 10, 2026

PigeonMail is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our application.

Information Collection & Use

PigeonMail requests access to your Google Account data via OAuth solely to display, search, and manage your emails within the client. We do not store, distribute, or share your personal data or email contents with any third parties. All processing is done locally on your device.

Data Security

We implement strict local security measures to protect your credentials and access tokens. Your tokens are stored securely and are only used to communicate directly with Google API servers.
